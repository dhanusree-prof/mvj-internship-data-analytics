# Project 1 — Business Recommendations

## 1. Encourage repeat purchasing

**Evidence:** The repeat customer rate is 72.35%, based on 5881 identifiable customers.

**Recommendation:** Test a targeted repeat-purchase campaign for customers who have purchased once but have not returned. Measure its success using the repeat customer rate and subsequent order frequency.

## 2. Review the leading products

**Evidence:** The top product by net line value is"Wonky bottom/broken" with stock code: 22423, with a net line value of 344563.25

**Recommendation:** Review stock availability and demand patterns for leading products before planning replenishment. Compare net line value with quantities and returns before making inventory decisions.

## 3. Investigate monthly sales changes

**Evidence:** The strongest positive MoM change occurred in 2010-03 based on the monthly analysis.

**Recommendation:** Investigate these months for product mix, order volumes, returns, and operational changes. Use the findings to guide future sales planning rather than assuming that the changes will repeat.

## 4. Monitor average order value

**Evidence:** The overall average order value is 459.1, calculated as the net sales proxy divided by distinct non-cancellation invoices.

**Recommendation:** Track AOV monthly and test relevant product bundles or complementary-product offers. Compare results against a baseline before deciding whether the offers are effective.

## 5. Improve sales reporting and data quality

**Evidence:** The analysis excludes cancellation invoices, retains negative quantities on other invoices, and cannot identify repeat behavior for customers without valid IDs.

**Recommendation:** Report cancellation and return effects separately in future analyses, monitor missing customer IDs, and document the calculation rules used for each KPI. This will make sales comparisons more reliable.

## Limitations

- Net line value is calculated from quantity multiplied by price and should not automatically be interpreted as official accounting revenue or profit.
- Repeat rate excludes customers without a valid customer ID.
- Monthly changes describe historical results and do not establish why sales changed.
- Recommendations should be tested and reviewed against subsequent business results.
