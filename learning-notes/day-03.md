# Day 3 — Excel Business Formulas and KPI Analysis

**Date:** 19 October 2026  
**Project:** Coffee Shop Sales KPI Analysis  
**Tool:** Microsoft Excel Online

## 1. Objective

To practise Excel business formulas and analyse coffee shop sales data using key performance indicators (KPIs).

## 2. Tasks Performed

- Downloaded the Maven Analytics Coffee Shop Sales dataset.
- Preserved an untouched copy of the original dataset.
- Created separate sheets for raw data, KPI summary, location analysis, monthly analysis, and formula practice.
- Calculated sales using transaction quantity and unit price.
- Practised Excel formulas including SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, IFERROR, EDATE, and XLOOKUP.
- Analysed sales by store location and month.
- Practised month-on-month growth calculations.

## 3. Key Learnings

- Used SUMIFS to calculate sales based on criteria.
- Used COUNTIFS to count records matching specified conditions.
- Used EDATE to generate consecutive monthly dates.
- Learned how to calculate average sales and month-on-month growth.
- Practised XLOOKUP to retrieve product information using product IDs.

## 4. Validation and Limitations

- Checked KPI calculations against the source data.
- Reviewed monthly sales results and investigated months showing zero sales.
- Checked XLOOKUP results against the corresponding source records.
- Year-on-year growth should only be reported when comparable data from consecutive years is available.
