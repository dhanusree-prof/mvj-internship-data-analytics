# Day 2: Types of Analytics and KPIs

## Concepts Learned
- Descriptive analytics: understanding what happened.
- Diagnostic analytics: investigating why it happened.
- Predictive analytics: estimating what may happen next.
- Prescriptive analytics: evaluating what action to take.
- KPI trees connect a high-level objective to measurable indicators.

## Practical Task
Created a KPI tree for Namma Metro covering ridership, peak share
and revenue proxies.

## Key Learning
A KPI must have a clear definition, a calculation method and a
business purpose. The required data must be available before the
KPI can be measured reliably.

## Limitations
The KPI tree defines proposed measures. Actual performance
conclusions require appropriate operational and financial data.

## AI Use
Used AI to help structure and explain the KPI framework.
Reviewed the formulas and documented the assumptions and limitations.
